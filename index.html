Who We Are

The IEEE Student Branch at Career Point University (CPU), Kota is a vibrant community of passionate engineering and technology students. We are dedicated to the advancement of technology for the benefit of humanity.

Our mission is to foster technological innovation and excellence by providing members with unparalleled opportunities to network, develop skills, and engage in cutting-edge technical projects.
